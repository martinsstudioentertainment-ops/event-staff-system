## Full description

```
Olasentra Staff is the official mobile app for approved event security and stewarding personnel.

Use the same account you registered with — sign in with Google or your registered email. Your existing profile, approvals, shift history, and documents stay linked to your account.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• View upcoming and completed shifts
• Check in at venues with GPS verification
• Open venue QR sign-in links when on site
• Read coordinator messages and notifications
• Update your profile and documents
• Install once on your home screen for fast access on shift days

BUILT FOR REAL SHIFTS
The app connects to the live Olasentra platform. When GPS attendance is enabled for an event, location is used only to confirm you are at the venue and to record attendance fairly.

REQUIREMENTS
• Approved staff registration with Olasentra
• Internet connection for sign-in and updates
• Location permission when GPS check-in is required for your event
• Notification permission (optional) for shift updates

Privacy policy: https://olasentra.com/privacy.php
Terms of use: https://olasentra.com/terms.php
```

## Data safety (Play Console form)

Declare collection consistent with Privacy Notice:

- Email, name, phone, DOB, PPS (staff registration)
- Bank details (payroll)
- Precise location (optional, event check-in only)
- App activity / install analytics (PWA install tracking)

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Users can request deletion via privacy contact.
